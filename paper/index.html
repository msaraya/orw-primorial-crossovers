Graded All-Orders Asymptotics for Successive-Primorial Crossovers in the Odlyzko–Rubinstein–Wolf Exponential-Integral Approximation

Abstract

Let P = pk# and q = pk+1, so that qP = pk+1# is the next primorial. We study the pairwise crossover between these two candidates in the explicit Odlyzko–Rubinstein–Wolf (ORW) exponential-integral approximation

𝓜(X,D) = 𝔖(D)ID(X),   ID(X) = ∫2X exp(−D/log t)/(log t)² dt.

Writing L = log X, we prove a uniform all-orders endpoint expansion for ID(eL) whose coefficients are j!Lj(1)(D/L), with remainder control stable under differentiation in the growing endpoint parameter. We then reorganize the logarithmic expansion into a graded asymptotic scale and rigorously invert the full crossover equation. If

L0 = (q−1)P / log((q−1)/(q−2))

is the exact solution of ORW's leading exponential comparison, then for every fixed M

Lint = L0 + Σm=0M cm(P,qP)/L0m + OM((1+qP)⌊(M+1)/2⌋/L0M+1),

where deg cm ≤ ⌊m/2⌋ and the coefficients are generated by an explicit Lagrange–Bürmann formula. In particular,

Lint = L0 + 1 + 3/L0 + O((1+qP)/L0²).

The corrections 1 and 3/L0 are universal for general endpoint pairs with D1/L0 → 0. The results are theorems about the stated ORW approximation; no unconditional claim about actual jumping champions is made.

Keywords: jumping champions; prime gaps; primorials; generalized Laguerre polynomials; asymptotic inversion.
